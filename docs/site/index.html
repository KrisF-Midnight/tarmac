Field Guide

tarmac & greeter

Seven chapters on one paved road — how a commit in an application repository becomes a running workload, and where the platform deliberately declines to hold a credential.

1 The app Dull on purpose. Its only job is to prove the road works, and to fail visibly when something behind it is broken. 2 CI Nine checks over one commit, in a fixed order, run by a program rather than a YAML script. 3 Policy The same rules twice — once before the merge, where a violation is a review comment, and again at admission. 4 Delivery One line of YAML, edited and pushed. A reconciler inside the cluster does everything after that. 5 Infrastructure One bucket and one object, declared by the platform rather than by the application that depends on them. 6 Local The whole road on a laptop with no cloud account — which buys reproducibility and costs you IAM. 7 Docs and omissions The reasoning, the absences, and the tables that are not allowed to go stale.
